# FemVoice Studio

FemVoice Studio is a Windows desktop app for voice training. It is designed to help users practice pitch, resonance, intonation, consistency, comfort, and sustainable training habits with real-time visual feedback and structured exercises.

The app runs locally on your PC. It is not a cloud service, and it is not a medical device.

## What It Does

- Shows real-time voice feedback from your microphone.
- Displays pitch and voice-training charts during practice.
- Provides structured exercises for pitch, resonance, intonation, breathing, and practical speech.
- Tracks training sessions and progress over time.
- Offers SmartCoach guidance based on your recent practice history.
- Includes voice health, recovery, pause, and comfort reminders.
- Supports light and dark themes.
- Includes report export tools for personal review or professional support.
- Stores data locally by default.

## Who It Is For

FemVoice Studio is intended for people who want a guided voice practice tool, especially users working on voice feminization. It can also be useful for structured self-practice, reflection, and tracking trends over time.

It should not replace a qualified clinician, speech-language therapist, or medical professional.

## Core Ideas

FemVoice Studio does not treat pitch as the only goal. Pitch is one part of a broader voice profile. The app also pays attention to:

- Resonance and formant placement.
- Vocal comfort and strain signals.
- Intonation and speech variation.
- Consistency across sessions.
- Recovery and rest.
- Sustainable progression.

The app is designed to discourage pushing, forcing, or chasing the highest possible pitch.

## Main Areas

### Dashboard

The dashboard is the main practice surface. It shows live pitch feedback, current training status, comfort-zone information, session controls, and quick navigation to other tools.

### Exercise Guide

The exercise guide contains structured practice activities. Exercises are grouped by focus area and difficulty so users can practice step by step.

### SmartCoach

SmartCoach provides daily or session-based guidance. It uses training history and safety signals to suggest what to focus on next.

### Analysis

The analysis windows show deeper charts and trends for pitch, resonance, intonation, and health-related signals. These charts are for training feedback, not diagnosis.

### Resonance Analysis

The resonance analysis window displays F1/F2 placement and formant timeline charts. It can help users inspect resonance-related patterns during practice.

### Progression

The progression view shows how training is developing over time and whether there is enough data to make meaningful progress estimates.

### Reports

The report tools can generate PDF, CSV, or JSON exports. These can be useful for reviewing progress or sharing selected summaries with a professional.

### Settings

Settings include theme, language, voice goals, accessibility preferences, microphone calibration, hearing your own voice, backup/restore, and database tools.

## Data And Privacy

FemVoice Studio is local-first. Training data and settings are stored on your computer. Support packages and exports are user-controlled.

Avoid sharing private notes, personal identifiers, or sensitive health information unless you intentionally choose to do so.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- A working microphone.
- A quiet practice environment.
- The packaged app build, or the .NET SDK if running from source.

## Important Safety Note

Stop or pause if you feel pain, strain, hoarseness, dizziness, or unusual discomfort. Use the app as a training aid, not as a diagnosis or medical assessment.

For clinical concerns, work with a qualified professional.
